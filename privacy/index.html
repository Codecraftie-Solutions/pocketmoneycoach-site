Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Pocket Money Coach ("the app") is made by Ignatius Sani ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: iggydevs@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't create an account. The app signs you in anonymously.
  • Your household, bills, claims, letter summaries and coach chats are stored encrypted on your phone, not on our servers.
  • When you use Snap it or the coach, we send what's needed to our AI provider to answer you. We don't keep your photos or messages.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads or use advertising IDs.

What stays on your phone

Your household profile (for example country, region, number of adults and children, working status, housing, benefits and payday), your bills, the support you're tracking and money you've got back, summaries of letters you've snapped, and your coach conversations. This data is encrypted on your device. You can download a copy or delete it at any time in Me, then Privacy and your data.

What we process on our servers

  • An anonymous user ID, created automatically so we can apply monthly limits and Pro access. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Usage counts: how many Snap it photos and coach messages you've used each month.
  • Subscription status: whether you have Pro, which product and when it expires, received from RevenueCat and the app store.
  • Organisation codes: if you enter a code from an organisation, we record that your anonymous ID belongs to that organisation.

Snap it and the coach

When you snap a bill or letter, the photo is sent to our server and passed to our AI provider, Anthropic, to read and explain it. We return a summary to your phone and do not store the photo.

When you message the coach, we send your recent messages and a short summary of your situation: country and region, currency, number of adults and children, whether anyone is pregnant, benefits, working status, housing tenure, pay frequency, your bill categories and amounts, and this week's summary. We do not send your name, address or children's exact birthdays.

Anthropic processes this data on our behalf and may keep it for a limited period under its own terms before deleting it. It does not use it to train its models. Photos and messages may include information about your health or finances, so only send what you're comfortable sharing.

Why we use your data

  • To provide the features you ask for, such as explaining a letter or answering a question: performance of a contract.
  • To apply free limits, provide Pro and prevent misuse of organisation codes: our legitimate interests.
  • Where you choose to share health details, such as pregnancy, with the coach: your explicit consent, which you can withdraw by not sharing them or by deleting your data.

Who we work with

  • Supabase: hosting, database and server functions.
  • Anthropic: AI that reads letters and answers coach messages.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store: payments and app distribution.

These providers process data only on our instructions. Some are based outside the UK, mainly in the United States. Where data is transferred, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Usage counts: 12 months.
  • Subscription status: while you have Pro, and for a reasonable period after for our records.
  • Photos and coach messages: not stored by us. Anthropic deletes them after its retention period.

Deleting your data

To delete everything on your phone, open the app and go to Me, then Privacy and your data, then Delete everything. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

To delete the anonymous record on our server (usage counts, subscription status and any organisation code), email iggydevs@gmail.com and we'll help you find and delete it, usually within 30 days. A Pro subscription is managed by your app store, so cancel it there.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to or restrict our use of it, and receive a copy. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

Data on your phone is encrypted, and you can turn on an app lock with a PIN, face or fingerprint. Data is encrypted in transit, and our database uses access rules so each anonymous ID can only reach its own records.

Children

The app is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. For significant changes we'll tell you in the app.
