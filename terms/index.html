What the app does

Pocket Money Coach helps you see what you can spend until payday, keep track of bills, find support you may be able to claim, get letters and bills explained, and ask a money coach questions.

The figures the app shows depend on what you enter. They are estimates to help you plan, not a statement of your finances.

The app, including the coach and Snap it, gives general information and guidance. It is not regulated financial advice, debt advice, legal advice or tax advice, and it doesn't recommend specific financial products, lenders or loans. For free, confidential debt advice, contact StepChange, National Debtline (0808 808 4000) or Citizens Advice.
