A money app for parents on tight budgets. See what you can spend until payday, find the support you're owed, and get bills and letters explained in plain English.

Try it on Android

Built for the UK. No account, no bank login.

One calm place for your money week.

Pocket Money Coach does the sums, keeps track of what's due and points you to help you might be missing.

Know what you can spend until payday

Tell it what you have and when you're paid. It takes off the bills that leave before then and shows what's left for each day.

  • Priority bills like rent, council tax and energy come first
  • One clear next step each week
  • A reminder the day before each bill leaves

Find money you're owed

Answer a few questions about your household and see the UK support you may be able to claim, each with a link to the official page.

  • Council tax discounts and reductions
  • Social tariffs for broadband and water
  • Healthy Start, free school meals and more

Snap a bill or letter, get it explained

Take a photo and get a plain-English summary: what it means, any deadline, what to do next and where to get free help. The photo is read, then deleted.

See which bills to pay first

Keep your bills and renewal dates in one list. The ones where missing a payment hurts most, like rent and energy, are marked to pay first.

Each bill has a short call script for asking for a better price or a social tariff.

Your money stays your business.

No sign-up, no bank connection, no ads.

Kept on your phone

Your household, bills, claims, letter summaries and coach chats are stored encrypted on your device.

Read, not kept

Letter photos and coach messages are sent to our AI provider only to be explained. We never store your photos.

Locked if you want

Add a PIN, face or fingerprint lock, hide your balance in the app switcher, and delete everything any time.
